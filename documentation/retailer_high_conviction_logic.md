# Retailer High-Conviction (Super-Safe) Logic Profile

This document defines the "Extremely Picky" criteria for identifying Super-Safe investment opportunities for retail investors. This mode is designed to prioritize capital preservation and high-probability success.

---

## 1. The "Super-Safe" Hard Filters

A stock must pass **ALL** of the following filters. Failure in even one metric results in an immediate disqualification from the High-Conviction status.

### A. Massive Financial Moat (Profitability)
- **Net Margin**: Must be **> 25%**. High margins indicate a strong competitive advantage and a "moat" that protects profits.
- **FCF Growth**: Free Cash Flow Growth must be **> 15% YoY**. This ensures the company is generating increasing amounts of real cash.

### B. Fortress Balance Sheet (Low Debt)
- **Debt-to-Equity (D/E)**: Must be **< 0.5**. The company must have very low leverage to withstand economic downturns.
- **Current Ratio**: Must be **> 1.5**. Ensures strong short-term liquidity.

### C. Big Money Backing (Institutional Alignment)
- **Institutional Ownership**: Must be **> 70%**. "Smart money" must have a significant stake, providing a floor for the stock price.

### D. Confirmed Uptrend (Technical Floor)
- **Trend Alignment**: The stock must be in a confirmed long-term uptrend: **Price > 50 SMA > 200 SMA**. This prevents "catching a falling knife."

### E. Absolute Wall Street Consensus
- **Analyst Ratings**: At least **90%** of analysts must have a 'Buy' or 'Strong Buy' rating. There should be almost no dissent among professional researchers.

## 3. The Verdict Tiers

- **Verdict: [SURE-WIN / RETAIL GOLD]**
  - **Requirement**: Passes ALL Hard Filters **AND** achieves a score of **95 or higher**.
  - *Thesis*: This is as safe as a stock trade gets. Total alignment of moat, money, and momentum.

- **Verdict: [HIGH CONVICTION BUY]**
  - **Requirement**: Passes ALL Hard Filters **AND** achieves a score of **85-94**.

- **Verdict: [STANDARD BUY]**
  - **Requirement**: Score of 75-84.

- **Verdict: [AVOID / NOT PICKY ENOUGH]**
  - **Requirement**: Score < 75 or fails any Hard Filter.

## 4. Retail Execution Strategy
- **Entry**: Only enter on minor pullbacks to the 50-day SMA to maximize safety.
- **Stop-Loss**: 5-7% below entry (Tight, as these stocks should not have high volatility).
- **Time Horizon**: 6-18 months (Allow the financial moat to manifest in price appreciation).
