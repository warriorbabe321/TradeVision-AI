# Expert Evaluation: The 20/50/200 SMA Triad Significance

## Overview
The combination of the 20-day, 50-day, and 200-day Simple Moving Averages (SMA) is a classic technical setup used by institutional and professional traders to identify trend alignment across multiple timeframes. Standardizing this setup is highly recommended for our "Sure-Win" retail strategy.

## The Significance of the Triad (Fast, Medium, Slow)

### 1. 20-Day SMA (The Fast Lead)
- **Role:** Short-term momentum and trend detection.
- **Significance:** It tracks the most recent price action. In a healthy "Sure-Win" setup, the price should stay above the 20-day SMA. A break below it is the first warning sign of momentum loss, while a bounce off it is a prime entry point for momentum traders.

### 2. 50-Day SMA (The Intermediate Support)
- **Role:** Intermediate-term trend and "Golden Cross" component.
- **Significance:** It acts as the "line in the sand" for institutional interest. Stocks in a strong bull market often find support at the 50-day SMA.

### 3. 200-Day SMA (The Secular Trend)
- **Role:** Long-term trend and institutional baseline.
- **Significance:** This is the most critical indicator for identifying a bull vs. bear market. Institutional buyers rarely buy stocks below the 200-day SMA. Price > 200 SMA is a mandatory filter for safety.

## Evaluation for the "Sure-Win" Strategy

Adding the 20-day SMA to our existing 50/200-day setup is **critical** for the following reasons:

1. **Trend Confirmation (The "Perfect Alignment"):** The highest conviction trades occur when the SMAs are stacked in order: **Price > 20 > 50 > 200**. This demonstrates that the trend is confirmed across short, medium, and long-term horizons simultaneously.
2. **Precision Entry Points:** While the 50 and 200 SMAs provide broad buy zones, the 20 SMA provides precise "swing" entries. A common high-conviction retail signal is a "pullback to the 20-day SMA" within a 50/200 bull trend.
3. **Early Exit Signal:** The 20 SMA serves as an effective trailing stop-loss baseline. If a "Sure-Win" stock breaks the 20-day SMA on high volume, it suggests the "Sure-Win" momentum is faltering, allowing for capital protection before the larger 50-day support is even tested.

## Recommendation: Final Verdict
**Yes, we should standardize the 20/50/200 setup in the app's charting and scoring.**

### Implementation Logic for Scoring:
- **Full Alignment (Price > 20 > 50 > 200):** Maximum score (100% Technical Trend Score).
- **Secondary Alignment (Price > 50 > 200 but Price < 20):** High score, but indicates a "pullback" state (Potential Buy the Dip).
- **Bearish Warning (Price < 50 or Price < 200):** Disqualify from "Sure-Win" status immediately.
