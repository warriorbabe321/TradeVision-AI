# Retailer High-Conviction (Sure-Win) Logic Profile

This document defines the criteria and scoring logic for the 'Retailer High-Conviction' (Sure-Win) verdict, designed to identify high-probability trades with strong institutional backing and technical support.

---

## 1. Mandatory Gatekeeping (The "Sure-Win" Filters)

A stock must pass **ALL** of the following filters to be even considered for a High-Conviction verdict. If any filter fails, the "Sure-Win" status is automatically disqualified.

### A. Core "No-Go" Checks (from Hedge Fund Hierarchy)
- **Debt-to-Equity**: Must be ≤ 2.0 (No debt crisis).
- **Net Margin**: Must not have had negative net margins for the last 2 consecutive quarters.
- **Regulatory Status**: Must have no active SEC investigations or major fraud allegations.
- **Liquidity**: Average Daily Volume must be ≥ $1M.

### B. Additional Safety Layers
- **Profitability Moat**: Net Margin must be > 20%.
- **Cash Flow Strength**: Free Cash Flow (FCF) Growth must be > 10% YoY.

### C. Institutional Alignment
- **"Big Money" Backing**: Institutional Ownership must be > 60%.

### D. Technical Floor
- **Long-Term Trend**: Stock price must be above its 200-day Simple Moving Average (200 SMA).

### E. Analyst Consensus
- **Wall Street Agreement**: At least 80% of total analyst ratings must be 'Buy' or 'Strong Buy'.

---

## 2. High Conviction Score (HC-Score) Calculation

The HC-Score is a 0-100 metric. While the gates above are binary (Pass/Fail), the score measures the *intensity* of the conviction.

| Category | Component | Scoring Logic | Max Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fundamentals** | Net Margin | >30% (15 pts), 20-30% (10 pts) | 15 |
| **Fundamentals** | FCF Growth | >20% (15 pts), 10-20% (10 pts) | 15 |
| **Institutions** | Ownership % | >80% (20 pts), 70-80% (15 pts), 60-70% (10 pts) | 20 |
| **Technicals** | Trend Alignment| Price > 50 SMA > 200 SMA (20 pts), Price > 200 SMA (10 pts) | 20 |
| **Consensus** | Buy/Strong Buy%| >95% (20 pts), 90-95% (15 pts), 80-90% (10 pts) | 20 |
| **Value/Upside**| Price Target | Upside > 20% (10 pts), 10-20% (5 pts) | 10 |
| **TOTAL** | | | **100** |

---

## 3. The "Sure-Win" Verdict Trigger

The final verdict is determined by the combination of the gates and the score.

- **Verdict: [SURE-WIN] (High Conviction)**
  - **Requirement**: Passes ALL filters in Section 1 **AND** achieves an HC-Score of **90 or higher**.
  - *Note*: This is the "Retailer's Dream" signal—everything is aligned.

- **Verdict: [STRONG BUY]**
  - **Requirement**: Achieves an HC-Score of 80-89 (even if one of the safety gates in Section 1 is slightly missed, provided No-Go checks pass).

- **Verdict: [BUY]**
  - **Requirement**: Achieves an HC-Score of 70-79.

- **Verdict: [SPECULATIVE / HOLD]**
  - **Requirement**: HC-Score < 70 or fails any core No-Go check.

---

## 4. Execution Guidance for Sure-Win Trades
- **Position Sizing**: Due to the high confidence, a larger-than-standard position size may be considered.
- **Stop-Loss**: Set at the 200 SMA or 8% below entry, whichever is tighter.
- **Thesis**: "Full institutional alignment with superior profitability and clear technical breakout."
